How KoLearn was made

A free Android app to learn Korean. The apps I tried were bloated with ads, so I made my own: easy for beginners, still useful as you progress.

Get it on Google Play All projects

How it grew

  1. 2017 – 2019

    Baeuran

    More of a learning experience. Java, since Kotlin was not widely known yet, and little thought for architecture.

  2. 2019 – 2020

    Baeuran and Learnion Kr

    Moved the whole codebase to Kotlin, started on Jetpack libraries and took design decisions more seriously.

  3. 2020 – 2021

    Learnion Kr, then KoLearn

    Settled on MVVM, Material Design, prototyping and testing.

  4. 2022 – 2024

    KoLearn

    Cohesive branding and UI. Every feature split into small tasks and prototyped in Adobe XD before code. Later moved from XML views to Jetpack Compose.

How it was built

UI and UX
Adobe XD for prototypes, XML views with data binding, Material Design as the guideline.
Development
Kotlin, Gradle, MVVM, Coroutines, Flows, Room, ML Kit, Paging, a single activity with fragments, WorkManager, LeakCanary.
Backend
Firebase Storage, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics, Auth, Functions and Test Lab; AdMob.

What's inside

Quizzes you shape

Timed, multiple choice or hand-drawn. Each quiz is built from what you have added, by priority, category or deck.

A summary at the end shows what you got right and wrong, and the points you earned.

Your own flashcards

Make cards in your own categories, with audio, an image or text on the front. Filter, search and change how they display.

Each card you miss in a quiz gets more priority, so the hard ones come back more often.

Lessons to start

Beginners learn to read and write basic Korean, with flashcards to add and quizzes for each topic.

The app tracks your progress and gives achievements at milestones.

A community

Offline first, with an optional account to chat with other learners: posts with images, shared flashcards and more.

Moderators can delete posts or block users.
